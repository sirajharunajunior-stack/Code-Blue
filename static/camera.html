Camera vitals

Heart rate and breathing are estimated in your browser; video stays on this device. When auto-save is on, measurements are sent to this server.

For the best reading
  • Face a window or lamp so your face is evenly lit.
  • Prop the phone up at arm’s length and keep it still.
  • Stay still and quiet for 30 seconds; don’t talk or smile.
  • Keep hair and glasses off your forehead and cheeks.
Save to backend
Readings are saved about every 10 seconds once the signal is good.

Not a medical device. Camera estimates can be off by several beats per minute, especially in dim light or with movement. Blood oxygen, blood pressure and temperature can’t be measured reliably with a normal camera. If you feel unwell, tell the crew medical officer whatever the numbers say.
